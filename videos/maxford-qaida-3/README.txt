Maxford Global Qaida 3+ English ki videos is folder mein rakhein.

Har letter ke liye yeh naam rakhein (a ki jagah letter):
  Story of Letter a.mp4        -> Sound page par 'Story' button
  Letter a Phonic Poem.mp4     -> Sound page par 'Phonic Poem' button
  Letter a Vocabulary.mp4      -> Sound page par 'Vocabulary' button
  Letter a Formation.mp4       -> Trace page par 'How to write a' button

Videos daalne ke baad Claude se kahein: 'Maxford ki videos laga do'
(ya chalayein: python tools/make_maxford.py)

Ya ek hi video jis mein likhna aur activities dono hon:
  Letter a Formation and Activities.mp4
  -> Claude video dekh kar har page (trace, trace-write, activities) par apna hissa lagata hai.
